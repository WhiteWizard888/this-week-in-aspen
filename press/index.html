Show #1 · September 22, 2026 · Belly Up, Aspen

Sponge + Candlebox live at Belly Up

A 90s throwback double bill in an intimate room — shot from the floor, review filed the same night.

Curated by Ron Delhaye

Doors, and the room is filling in around a blue-lit empty stage. Belly Up at its best — small enough that there's no bad spot, anticipation doing half the work before a note is played.

Sponge opened and tore through it — awesome, full stop. Then Candlebox, deep into their 30th year, took the room apart song by song: arms-raised singalongs, a frontman in shades giving everything to the back row, and a crowd that came to party like it was 1995.

"Underrated winner!! Total throwback — amazing performance and energy by both bands. Feel like I'm partying in 1995."

Show #1 — the first entry in our published live-music portfolio. More to come.
